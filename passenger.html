Passenger Identity & Transit

HarborLink Partners redefines the passenger experience by deploying the Bureau of Digital Identity to high-traffic maritime transit hubs. By utilizing REALAiiD Wallet "Bridges," we transform the passenger journey from a series of friction-heavy checkpoints into a seamless, biometric-validated flow.

Wallet "Bridge" Functionality

Passengers carrying a REALAiiD Wallet with loaded "Bridges" gain instant, high-assurance mobility:

  • Cryptographic Boarding: Replace physical boarding passes with a time-locked, encrypted "Travel Bridge" that validates identity and ticket status in milliseconds.
  • Biometric Expediting: Use local, on-device biometric handshakes to clear security perimeters without showing physical identification documents.
  • Unified Experience: Access facility amenities (lounges, concessions, valet) by simply interacting with authorized Bridge-readers, linking digital identity to service provisioning.

Forensic Transit Integrity

For terminal operators, our passenger protocols provide unmatched oversight:

  • Real-Time Manifest Sync: Immediate, accurate data on current passenger location and terminal occupancy.
  • Forensic Audit Trail: Every passenger interaction is cryptographically logged in our forensic data-vault, ensuring absolute compliance with maritime safety and federal transit mandates.
  • Anomaly Detection: Automated identification of irregular transit patterns or expired credentials, allowing for proactive intervention.
