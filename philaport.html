PhilaPort Cruise Terminal Opportunity

Philadelphia, PA — Norwegian Cruise Line Homeport

We are excited to present our capabilities and interest in supporting this flagship project

Project Interest

HarborLink Partners is actively pursuing opportunities to support the new 16-acre PhilaPort Cruise Terminal at 4 Atlantic Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19153. This important development, with marine-side improvements by PhilaPort and land-side terminal development by Norwegian Cruise Line (NCL), represents a significant opportunity for advanced technology implementation.

Our Interest & Value Proposition

We are well-positioned to deliver comprehensive IT infrastructure, Identity & Access Management, security systems, and passenger processing solutions that will enhance operations during the current construction phase and support long-term success once fully operational.

Bureau of Digital Identity at PhilaPort

Powered by REALAiiD, Inc., our Bureau of Digital Identity platform offers a modern, privacy-first approach to credentialing and access control. This standards-compliant ecosystem is designed to meet stringent maritime security, forensic audit, and data protection requirements.

If users load their verified credentials (“bridges”) into the REALAiiD Wallet, they will be able to:

  • Perform instant, contactless biometric verification at security checkpoints
  • Receive digital boarding passes and real-time operational clearances
  • Securely share TWIC, passport, visa, and maritime certifications
  • Enable role-based access control for crew, contractors, and vendors
  • Experience significantly accelerated embarkation and debarkation
  • Maintain a forensically auditable digital trail for compliance
  • Support seamless integration with CBP and NCL systems
  • Reduce queue times and improve overall passenger experience
  • Strengthen the terminal’s security posture
