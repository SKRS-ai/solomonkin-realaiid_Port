PhilaPort Cruise Terminal

Philadelphia, PA — Norwegian Cruise Line Homeport

Delivering secure, intelligent technology solutions for a world-class cruise facility

Project Overview

The new 16-acre PhilaPort Cruise Terminal at 4 Atlantic Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19153, is a flagship development. PhilaPort leads marine-side improvements while Norwegian Cruise Line (NCL) develops the land-side terminal under a 7-year exclusive berthing agreement.

Currently in active construction with live cruise operations (including Norwegian Jewel sailings), this project presents unique opportunities for phased technology deployment.

Our Role at PhilaPort

We provide comprehensive IT infrastructure, advanced Identity & Access Management, security systems, and passenger processing solutions tailored to the challenges of operating a busy cruise terminal during construction.

Bureau of Digital Identity at PhilaPort

Powered by REALAiiD, Inc., our Bureau of Digital Identity platform brings next-generation digital credentialing to the port environment. This privacy-first, standards-compliant ecosystem meets stringent maritime security, forensic audit, and data protection requirements.

If users load their verified credentials (“bridges”) into the REALAiiD Wallet, they will be able to:

  • Perform instant, contactless biometric verification at terminal entry and security checkpoints
  • Receive digital boarding passes and real-time operational clearances
  • Seamlessly share TWIC, passport, visa, and maritime certifications with port authorities
  • Enable role-based access for crew, contractors, and vendors with automated revocation
  • Experience accelerated embarkation and debarkation processes
  • Maintain a forensically auditable, tamper-proof digital trail for all access events
  • Support CBP (Customs & Border Protection) integration and compliance
  • Reduce physical document handling and queue times
  • Enhance overall security posture while improving passenger and crew experience
