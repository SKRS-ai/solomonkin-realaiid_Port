Maritime Security & Regulatory Compliance

HarborLink Partners delivers security infrastructure specifically engineered to satisfy the rigorous demands of the Maritime Transportation Security Act (MTSA) and 33 CFR Part 105. Our systems provide the automated assurance required by modern port authorities to maintain secure, restricted-access facility status.

33 CFR Part 105 Compliance

Our solutions provide the required Facility Security Plan (FSP) support through automated access control and continuous monitoring. We ensure that only cleared personnel gain entry to restricted areas, with full biometric authentication logs.

Hardened Air-Gap Capability

The core of our hardware is an air-gapped cryptographic architecture. By physically isolating identity-verification keys from the public-facing network, we eliminate the primary vector for remote cyber-intrusion, ensuring terminal security even during network-wide outages.

The REALAiiD Wallet: Operational Authority

When personnel bridge their REALAiiD Wallet to our terminal nodes, the system executes high-assurance protocol checks:

  • Identity Validation: Cryptographic verification of TWIC and port credentials in milliseconds.
  • Zero-Knowledge Access: Authorization is confirmed without exposing underlying PII, preserving privacy while meeting security mandates.
  • Forensic Audit Trail: Every ingress event creates a tamper-proof record, immediately available for USCG or port authority audit compliance.
