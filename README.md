## Overview

**HarborLink Partners** is the dedicated Port Solutions division of Solomon Kin LLC and REALAiiD, Inc. We provide specialized **IT Infrastructure, Identity & Access Management (IAM), Security Systems, and Operational Support** for modern cruise terminals.

We are actively positioning for opportunities at the **PhilaPort Cruise Terminal** (4 Atlantic Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19153) — the new 16-acre homeport for Norwegian Cruise Line (NCL). With marine-side improvements led by PhilaPort and land-side development by NCL, the facility is currently supporting live cruise operations (including Norwegian Jewel sailings) while construction continues. Our phased approach delivers immediate temporary solutions during build-out and seamless transition to full permanent infrastructure.
