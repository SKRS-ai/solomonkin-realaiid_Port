Identity & Access Management (IAM)

The Bureau of Digital Identity provides a high-assurance, cryptographic environment where identity is no longer a static document, but a dynamic, verifiable asset. By utilizing the REALAiiD Wallet, users maintain absolute data sovereignty while engaging with port infrastructures.

The Bridge Architecture: What the Wallet Can Do

When a user loads "Bridges" into their REALAiiD Wallet, they are not merely carrying credentials; they are deploying authorized agents for secure interaction:

  • Biometric Synchronization: Instantly verify identity across port terminals without physical tokens, using encrypted, time-locked biometric handshake protocols.
  • Automated Compliance: Automatically transmit specific, redacted certifications (e.g., TWIC, HAZMAT, or site-safety training) to facility readers, granting or denying entry based on real-time eligibility.
  • Forensic Tracking: Generate an immutable, timestamped audit trail of every access interaction, stored within the REALAiiD ecosystem for post-event forensic analysis.
  • Dynamic Access Bridging: Securely grant temporary facility access to contractors by "bridging" permissions directly from the central Bureau to their unique hardware device.

Forensic Integrity & Ecosystem Security

Our ecosystem is engineered to prevent unauthorized access at the kernel level. By utilizing air-gapped cryptographic signing and decentralized verification, we ensure that the identity of the person interacting with the facility is indisputable and cryptographically bound to the transaction.
