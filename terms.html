Terms & Forensic Governance

HarborLink Partners (Solomon Kin LLC & REALAiiD, Inc.) mandates these Terms of Service to ensure the integrity, security, and forensic accountability of the Bureau of Digital Identity ecosystem.

1. The Bridge-Wallet Protocol

By loading credentials into the REALAiiD Wallet, the User authorizes the "Bridge" to interact with authorized port nodes. This activation creates a localized, cryptographically-signed audit event. Users acknowledge that these Bridges facilitate:

  • Biometric Synchronization: Localized identity verification via secure hardware-backed tokens.
  • Automated Compliance Verification: Real-time validation of regulatory status (TWIC/MARSEC) against federal standards.
  • Forensic Traceability: Immutable logging of all access-event metadata for post-incident analysis and system audit requirements.

2. Liability & Forensic Accuracy

HarborLink Partners guarantees the cryptographic security of the platform. Users acknowledge that the immutable forensic logs generated by the Bureau serve as the authoritative evidence for all system transactions. Misuse of the REALAiiD Wallet or unauthorized attempts to spoof Bridge-signing protocols constitute a breach of service and immediate termination of access.

3. Data Sovereignty Mandate

Our ecosystem respects User Identity Sovereignty. No personal identity data is stored in centralized repositories by HarborLink Partners. User authorization is required for every forensic audit request, ensuring that digital interaction is always compliant with the principle of least privilege.
