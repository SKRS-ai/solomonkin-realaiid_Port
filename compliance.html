Compliance & Regulatory Integrity

HarborLink Partners (Solomon Kin LLC & REALAiiD, Inc.) operates under a Zero-Trust mandate. Our adherence to federal and international maritime security standards is not an overlay; it is the native architecture of our Bureau of Digital Identity.

The Wallet-Bridge Compliance Advantage

When personnel load official credentials into their REALAiiD Wallet, these "Bridges" become cryptographically verified proof-of-compliance. This enables automated audit-readiness:

  • Automated Credential Verification: Instantly check certifications (TWIC, HAZMAT, etc.) against federal databases at every access point.
  • Immutable Forensic Trails: Every regulatory check is logged as a tamper-proof event, creating a perfect audit history for federal investigators.
  • Automated Revocation: Immediate, global suspension of Bridge access if a certification expires or if a threat-vector is identified, ensuring continuous compliance.

Federal Maritime Standards

Our solutions align with MARSEC levels, NIST cybersecurity frameworks, and TSA access control protocols. We provide the forensic depth necessary to satisfy the most stringent government oversight requirements.

Data Sovereignty & Privacy

We prioritize the individual’s digital ownership. The Bureau’s protocol ensures that biometric and personal data are never stored in centralized silos, adhering to the highest global data protection standards (GDPR, CCPA, and beyond).
