About HarborLink Partners

HarborLink Partners is the specialized Port Solutions division of Solomon Kin LLC and REALAiiD, Inc.. We combine deep maritime operational expertise with cutting-edge IT infrastructure and digital identity technologies to deliver secure, efficient, and future-ready solutions for modern cruise terminals and port facilities.

The REALAiiD Digital Identity Ecosystem

At the core of our innovation is the Bureau of Digital Identity — powered by REALAiiD, Inc. This secure, privacy-first digital wallet ecosystem allows individuals and organizations to manage verified digital credentials with military-grade security and seamless interoperability.

Bureau of Digital Identity

If you load your verified credentials (bridges) into your REALAiiD Wallet, you can:

  • Instantly authenticate at port security checkpoints using biometric + digital credential verification
  • Streamline crew and contractor access control with role-based permissions
  • Enable contactless passenger embarkation and debarkation
  • Securely share TWIC, passport, visa, and maritime certifications
  • Receive real-time operational clearances and digital boarding passes
  • Maintain a verifiable audit trail for compliance and incident response

Our Maritime Focus
