Cargo-Bound Protocol

The Cargo-Bound Protocol represents the final evolution of logistics security. By replacing fragile, paper-based bills of lading with cryptographically signed "Bound-State" records, we ensure that cargo is inseparable from its authorized handler. When an identity bridge is loaded into the REALAiiD Wallet, it creates a secure, digital tether between the freight and the operator.

Operational Capabilities of the Cargo-Bound Wallet

When personnel load their Bridges into the REALAiiD Wallet, they gain the power to manage high-value freight with absolute integrity:

  • Atomic Binding: Cargo is digitally "chained" to the driver’s identity. The shipment physically cannot be released from a facility gate unless the REALAiiD Wallet broadcasts a valid, cryptographically signed "Release Bridge."
  • Forensic Hand-offs: Every time cargo changes hands, the Wallet records the biometric signature of both parties, creating a non-repudiable ledger of the exchange.
  • Localized Authentication: The wallet performs air-gapped cryptographic handshakes with terminal readers, ensuring that freight is only accessible to personnel with the requisite safety and security clearances.

Anti-Spoofing Architecture

Our platform eliminates the "fictitious pickup" risk. Because the Cargo-Bound protocol validates the driver's identity in real-time against immutable Bureau records, impostors—no matter how convincing—cannot trigger the release of cargo.

Real-Time Forensic Audit

The status of every piece of cargo is updated instantly as it moves through the supply chain. This provides shipping owners with a "forensically covert" view of their goods, detecting unauthorized deviations or terminal dwell-time anomalies before they become claims.
