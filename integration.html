Technical Forensic Architecture

By leveraging an identity-driven orchestration layer, HarborLink ensures that all data movement between vendors is minimized and protected. Key integration pillars include:

  • Immutable Ledger Reconciliation: Any discrepancy between physical arrival at a dcs.aero kiosk and the cruisePAL operational manifest triggers a silent, forensically secure alert to the Bureau.
  • Zero-Trust Hardware Nodes: dcs.aero terminals function as isolated trust zones. Data is encrypted at the edge; only cryptographic proofs (not PII) are transmitted to the backend.
  • Unified Cryptographic Key Management: Both cruisePAL and dcs.aero endpoints share a hardened, rotate-on-demand key infrastructure governed by the REALAiiD protocol.
