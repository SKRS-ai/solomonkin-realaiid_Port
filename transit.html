Secure Transit Protocol

Secure Transit is the lifeblood of global trade. HarborLink Partners optimizes this flow by replacing legacy, vulnerable checkpoints with the Bureau of Digital Identity’s unified verification framework. By moving data through the REALAiiD Wallet, we ensure that every transit event is cryptographically signed and forensically tracked.

Unified Transit Authorization

With their REALAiiD Wallet, operators carry a unified credential set (Bridges). These Bridges allow for seamless hand-offs between vessel operators, truck drivers, and terminal managers, ensuring the integrity of the manifest remains unbroken from origin to destination.

Forensic Event Signing

Transit is not just movement; it is a series of verifiable data events. Our protocol signs every movement event to an immutable ledger, providing a forensically covert audit trail that identifies bottlenecks and prevents illicit cargo intrusion.

Bridge Functionality for Transit

  • Credential "Binding": Automatically bind high-value cargo manifests to the driver’s digital identity vault.
  • Zero-Trust Ingress: Enable automated transit of goods through terminals using localized, air-gapped cryptographic handshakes.
  • Real-time Compliance: Instantly update the shipping manifest’s status against international maritime security databases (MARSEC/AIS).
