Bureau of Digital Identity & IAM

Powered by REALAiiD’s secure digital wallet ecosystem.

If credentials (“bridges”) are loaded into a REALAiiD Wallet, users can:

  • Perform instant biometric + digital credential verification at security checkpoints
  • Receive contactless, paperless boarding passes and operational clearances
  • Securely share verified TWIC, passport, visas, and maritime certifications
  • Enable role-based access control for crew, contractors, and vendors
  • Maintain a forensically auditable, tamper-proof digital trail for compliance
  • Streamline embarkation/debarkation with zero-contact processing
  • Support real-time revocation and status updates from port authorities
