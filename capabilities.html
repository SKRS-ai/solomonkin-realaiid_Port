Operational Capabilities Statement

HarborLink Partners (Solomon Kin LLC & REALAiiD, Inc.) delivers a unified digital ecosystem for port authorities, focusing on secure access, infrastructure integrity, and optimized throughput.

The Bureau of Digital Identity

The Bureau acts as the central authority for our cryptographic identity framework. It provides the backbone for the REALAiiD Wallet, allowing personnel and logistics operators to carry verified digital "Bridges."

Wallet "Bridges" in Action

Once a user loads credentials (Bridges) into their REALAiiD Wallet, they gain instant, contactless capability to:

  • Secure Access: Pass through biometric-validated turnstiles and gates without manual badge verification.
  • Dynamic Compliance: Transmit real-time health, safety, and certification data to port security systems.
  • Asset Interaction: Authorize machinery operation or terminal entry by interacting with IoT-enabled "Bridge" readers.

Infrastructure & Security

Our solutions integrate seamlessly with legacy terminal operating systems (TOS). We offer end-to-end encryption, multi-factor authorization, and immutable audit logs that meet federal cybersecurity standards for critical infrastructure.

Strategic Procurement Commitment

We are well-positioned to support PhilaPort’s modernization goals. Our framework ensures that operational security never hinders logistic flow. By utilizing our platform, port leadership gains forensic-level visibility into all human and vehicle movements across the facility.
