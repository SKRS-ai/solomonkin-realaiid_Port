Immutable Forensic Audit Protocol

In high-stakes maritime environments, accountability is the ultimate security metric. The Bureau of Digital Identity utilizes an immutable, cryptographic logging system that ensures every interaction—from a simple gate-swipe to a complex hazardous material manifest—is non-repudiable and forensically accessible.

REALAiiD Wallet: The Audit Witness

When Bridges (authorized credentials) are loaded into the REALAiiD Wallet, they function as independent logging nodes:

  • Cryptographic Witnessing: Every time a user interacts with a physical reader, their Wallet generates a signed, timestamped manifest of the interaction.
  • Non-Repudiable Logs: Because these logs are cryptographically bound to the user's unique identity "Bridge," they cannot be altered or deleted, satisfying strict federal compliance mandates.
  • Post-Event Forensic Reconstruction: In the event of a security inquiry, administrators can reconstruct the exact sequence of events, identifying unauthorized shifts or anomalous movement patterns with granular precision.

Compliance & Governance

Our platform automates audit-readiness. By feeding real-time identity data into a secure, immutable ledger, we allow facility managers to produce compliance reports for internal security reviews or federal regulatory bodies on demand.

Forensically Covert Monitoring

Our system logs metadata silently and securely at the edge. This provides complete visibility into port activity without interfering with the speed of logistics or the daily throughput of cargo operations.
