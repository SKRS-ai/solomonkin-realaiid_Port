MARSEC Governance & Security

HarborLink Partners integrates seamlessly with MARSEC (Maritime Security) levels, providing an automated, forensically hardened framework for port ingress and operational integrity. We ensure that your facility remains compliant with USCG standards while optimizing for modern, high-threat-vector environments.

Bridge-Node Integration for MARSEC

By loading official MARSEC credential "Bridges" into the REALAiiD Wallet, authorized personnel and assets gain immediate, cryptographically secured capabilities:

  • MARSEC-Adjustable Access Logic: Automatically adjust security posture (from Level 1 to Level 3) in real-time across the entire port-wide node network.
  • Automated Credential Rotation: Cryptographically expire and re-issue facility-access tokens as threat-level mandates shift, ensuring zero stale access.
  • Forensic Perimeter Hardening: Every interaction with a gate or vessel is logged in an immutable, timestamped record, establishing a 100% auditable chain-of-custody.
  • Biometric Identity Validation: Ensure that every Bridge interaction is tied to a verified biometric signature, preventing credential spoofing or physical key-sharing.

Compliance & Oversight

Our platform provides comprehensive reporting tools designed for USCG audits. By centralizing the Bureau’s forensic data, terminal operators gain a single pane of glass for all security-related access events, streamlining regulatory reporting and enhancing internal security visibility.

Hardened Infrastructure

We believe in "Security by Design." The Bureau Protocol operates on an air-gapped-ready, zero-trust foundation, ensuring your MARSEC integrity isn't dependent on potentially vulnerable network backbones or external dependencies.
