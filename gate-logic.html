Gate Logic: Forensic Ingress

The "Gate Logic" protocol is the active enforcement layer of the Bureau of Digital Identity. It transforms a standard transit point into a Zero-Trust Forensic Gateway, where every entity—vessel, cargo, or individual—must perform a cryptographic handshake before physical access is granted.

How Bridges Activate Ingress

When an identity-enabled asset approaches a gate, the REALAiiD Wallet initiates a secure, air-gapped exchange:

  • Challenge-Response Authentication: The Gate Node issues a temporal cryptographic challenge. The Wallet responds with a signed proof of authorization.
  • Contextual Validation: The system verifies not just the identity, but current MARSEC compliance status, manifest integrity, and biometric verification tokens.
  • Automated Clearing: Once validated, the gate grants instantaneous, non-repudiable access, logging the event as a permanent "Bridge" record.

Hardened Security Posture

Our gate architecture eliminates "implied trust." Even if a gate is networked, the logic remains local and air-gapped from the broader administrative layer, preventing lateral movement and securing the perimeter against digital exploitation.

Data Sovereignty at the Edge

By keeping the verification logic at the "edge" (the physical gate), we ensure that sensitive user data remains with the holder, verified by the system without the need for mass-database exposure.
