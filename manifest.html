Living Manifest Protocol

The traditional shipping manifest is a point-in-time document, prone to tampering and error. HarborLink Partners replaces this with the Living Manifest—a continuously updated, cryptographically signed data object that resides in the Bureau of Digital Identity ecosystem.

REALAiiD Wallet Integration

When a shipping manifest is loaded into the REALAiiD Wallet, it becomes an active asset:

  • Atomic Binding: Every cargo item is cryptographically "bound" to the transport container and the driver's identity, ensuring non-repudiation during hand-offs.
  • Forensic Reconciliation: The Wallet automatically reconciles the physical manifest against incoming sensor data (e.g., weight, temperature, radiation), flagging discrepancies in milliseconds.
  • Automated Customs Handshake: Upon entering a secure zone, the Wallet pushes the manifest data to authorities via an air-gapped handshake, eliminating manual paperwork delays.

Automated Compliance

Our manifest system automatically cross-references international shipping regulations. If a shipment contains restricted goods, the "Bridge" to that specific manifest will only activate if the handler has verified hazardous material certifications.

Provenance Tracking

Every change to the manifest—from item scanning to seal verification—is recorded on an immutable ledger. This provides a perfect, forensically covert audit trail of who touched which cargo, where, and when.
