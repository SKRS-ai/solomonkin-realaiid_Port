The Bridge Vault Protocol

The Bridge Vault is the hardened repository within the Bureau of Digital Identity where high-assurance credentials are provisioned. Once a Bridge is loaded into a REALAiiD Wallet, it acts as a cryptographically signed key that synchronizes the individual with the port’s infrastructure.

Bridge Wallet Activation

By loading these Bridges, personnel gain instant, automated capability across the port ecosystem:

  • Biometric Asset Binding: The Bridge is physically bound to the user via biometric hardware signatures, ensuring non-repudiation.
  • Automated Forensic Handshaking: Bridges perform instantaneous authorization checks at gates, loading bays, and server room access points with zero-latency.
  • Cryptographic Revocation: The Vault allows the Bureau to push global revocation signals to any Bridge, neutralizing credentials the moment access is terminated.
  • Sovereign Interaction: Each Bridge allows the wallet to provide necessary credentials to authorized sensors without transmitting the user's master identity profile.

Forensic Integrity

Every interaction involving a Bridge-Node generates an immutable, forensic-grade record. This provides auditors with a perfect reconstruction of every digital movement within the harbor.

Hardened Security

The Vault operates under a Zero-Trust architecture. Bridges are never stored in the clear and are dynamically rotated to prevent potential signal-interception or replay attacks.
