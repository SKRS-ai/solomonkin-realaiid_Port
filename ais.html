AIS Domain Awareness Protocol

The Automatic Identification System (AIS) is the heartbeat of maritime positioning. At the Bureau of Digital Identity, we treat AIS data not as mere telemetry, but as a critical security input. By fusing AIS signals with the REALAiiD ecosystem, we turn real-time vessel movement into actionable security triggers.

REALAiiD Wallet: The AIS Logic Node

When vessel manifests are synchronized with REALAiiD, the Wallet performs real-time verification against global AIS feeds:

  • Automated Anomaly Detection: The system automatically flags "Dark Ships" (vessels that disable their AIS transponders) and cross-references them against your secure facility's pre-approved movement list.
  • Geo-Fencing & Gate Automation: Your REALAiiD Wallet triggers automated gate opening sequences only when a vessel’s AIS position is verified within the designated arrival window, ensuring seamless, hands-free terminal ingress.
  • Forensic Tracking: Historical AIS data is archived within the Bureau’s immutable ledger, providing a forensically covert audit trail of vessel arrival, berthing, and cargo discharge patterns.

Enhanced Maritime Domain Awareness

We fuse AIS telemetry with biometric handler data. This ensures that the vessel identified by AIS matches the crew manifesting through your gates, effectively closing the gap between digital ship positioning and physical human verification.

Compliance & Sanctions Enforcement

Our platform instantly alerts security teams if a vessel identified in the AIS feed has recent call-port history linked to sanctioned or high-risk territories, automating compliance verification before the vessel even arrives at the berth.
