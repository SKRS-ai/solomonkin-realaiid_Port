Forensic Threat Intelligence

In the modern maritime landscape, information is the primary vector of both attack and defense. The Bureau of Digital Identity maintains a continuous **Threat Posture** that translates global signals into local facility-level actions.

REALAiiD Wallet: The Defensive Edge

When personnel and assets are integrated into the REALAiiD ecosystem, the Wallet acts as a real-time security node:

  • Automated MARSEC Rotation: As global threat levels shift, your Wallet Bridges automatically update credential requirements, enforcing new security hurdles without administrative downtime.
  • Cryptographic Blacklisting: Revoke access across all facility gates instantly if an identity credential is flagged by Bureau intelligence.
  • Anomaly Detection: Our "Forensic Covert" logging identifies irregular access patterns, such as off-peak ingress attempts, and elevates threat priority before a breach can escalate.

Immutable Forensic Audit

Every threat detected—whether a spoofed credential or a unauthorized transit attempt—is recorded as an immutable ledger entry. This prevents "data-wiping" after an incident and provides forensic proof for federal investigators.

Dynamic Threat Response

Our platform does not just log threats; it acts. The REALAiiD ecosystem facilitates dynamic "safety zoning," where access permissions are micro-segmented based on current threat intelligence feeds, shielding high-value cargo zones.
