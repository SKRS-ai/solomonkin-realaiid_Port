Privacy & Data Sovereignty

At HarborLink Partners (Solomon Kin LLC & REALAiiD, Inc.), we reject the paradigm of centralized data collection. Our philosophy is rooted in Identity Sovereignty: the belief that your digital identity belongs to you, and should only be shared through explicit, cryptographic consent.

The Wallet-Bridge Privacy Protocol

By loading "Bridges" into your REALAiiD Wallet, you gain granular control over your digital footprint:

  • Selective Disclosure: When interacting with port infrastructure, you choose exactly which data attributes to share (e.g., verifying you are authorized without sharing who you are or your full record).
  • Local Identity Vaulting: All identity-based cryptographic keys reside on your hardware device. The Bureau of Digital Identity authenticates the transaction without ever storing or viewing your personal data.
  • Ephemeral Interaction: Once a Bridge transaction is validated, the session closes. No lingering data tracks are left on the reader, ensuring your privacy is maintained across all terminal environments.

Forensic Compliance without Surveillance

Our forensic data-vaults are designed for compliance, not observation. We log transactions, not people. By binding interactions to an immutable ledger while keeping personal identity attributes encrypted and off-chain, we provide port authorities with the audit-ready transparency they require, while guaranteeing that the privacy of every individual is protected at the kernel level.
