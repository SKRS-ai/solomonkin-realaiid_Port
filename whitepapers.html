Technical Documentation & Whitepapers

The Bureau of Digital Identity operates on a foundation of rigorous scientific and cryptographic principle. This repository hosts our technical whitepapers, providing the depth required for engineers, security auditors, and stakeholders to evaluate the efficacy of our REALAiiD Wallet architecture and the "Bridge" protocol.

REALAiiD Wallet: Understanding the Protocol

When you utilize our whitepapers, you gain the "forensically covert" insight required to implement our identity ecosystem. These documents detail:

  • Cryptography Lifecycle: A deep dive into how "Bridges" are generated, authenticated, and stored within the air-gapped environment of the REALAiiD Wallet.
  • Systems Architecture: Technical breakdowns of how decentralized identity nodes communicate and verify data integrity without relying on external, vulnerable cloud-based servers.
  • Forensic Auditability: Documentation of our non-repudiable transaction logging system, demonstrating how every interaction creates a permanent, tamper-proof record for security oversight.
